# Changelog

What changed in Lotus Altar, newest first. Each push to `main` deploys to Vercel, so dates stand in for versions.
For working notes and open questions, see `CONTINUUM.md`.

## 2026-11-07

### Added
- Dragon and lion dance training on the kung fu ground in the 30 days before Chinese New Year: the dragon from 6 to 9 AM and 9 to 10 PM,
  the lions from 9:30 to 11 AM and 6 to 8 PM (Bangkok time), with a drummer and cymbal player.

## 2026-11-04

### Added
- A row of houses across the road from the township football ground, so that side of the road isn't empty.
- A bamboo fence with a gate round each hut's yard in the village in front of the hospital.
- A waterfall where the stream rises, west of the fields behind the hospital: it falls off a rock cliff into a boulder-ringed pool, with spray and its own sound.

### Removed
- The zoom in / zoom out buttons. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

## 2026-11-03

### Added
- The Shan monastery's shrine hall opens, with a Shan-style Buddha; its stair joins the hill stair.
- A paved plinth lets walkers circle the Shan monastery.
- The stupa and monastery carry name boards in Shan.
- Visitors can step inside the Kachin Baptist church.
- The Haw Kham, the saopha's palace, east of the stupa hill, with a lane to it and a stair from it up the hill.
- Five two-storey Shan houses facing the Haw Kham.
- The street runs on west and north to the hospital's forecourt.
- A small township football ground across the road from the hospital.

### Changed
- Every lamp goes out by day, and daylight comes into the halls.
- The stupa hill's entrance gate is raised so its name board stands over the stair.
- The Shan image hall is dressed the Shan way: red-and-gold back wall, blue halo, gilt altar. Offerings stand on the
  throne's ledge instead of a table.
- The Shan Buddha's head is bare: dark curls and a small gilt bud, no jewelled band.
- The night sky is black.
- The title and location line no longer show at the top of the screen.

### Fixed
- The gap at the top of the monastery stair is closed, so walkers can step onto the verandah.
- The monastery verandah's back railing has balusters like its sides and front.
- The festival ground's flag pole no longer crowds the pavilion steps.

### Removed
- Trees and shrubs on the path to the main stupa.
- The foothill that stood across the road to the hospital.

## 2026-11-02

### Added
- An offering altar (water, light, incense, fruit) at every statue.
- 5F becomes a Thousand-Armed Guanyin shop; 6F becomes a Victorious Buddha hall.
- A free-standing rack of lamps and candles on 2F.
- Shan script is accepted alongside Burmese for signs and boards.

### Fixed
- The top floor's stairwell rail stops at the stair room's back wall and ends on posts clear of it; the top step of
  the 7F stairs is clear.

## 2026-10-29

### Added
- Townsfolk at the night market and on the roads, in sarongs and sashes, dressed warmly for cold nights, with
  umbrellas in the rain. They go home at 10 PM and the market's lights go out.
- A beat policeman who chased walkers outside the temple and markets at night. He is now switched off.

## 2026-10-28

### Added
- Weather over the northern hills (clear, cloudy, rain, mist, haze) and the sounds of the world outside.
- A torch for walking outside at night.
- A bamboo grove behind the school, with houses facing its gate.
- A Shan-style welcome board for Hsenwi on the verge beside the police station.
- A Kachin Baptist church past the plaza lane, with six houses round it.

### Changed
- The wind comes in soft gusts with long stillness between; the banyan and the fields blow once, then lie still for
  about 15 minutes. The old banyan breathes a faint moan at night.

### Removed
- The sound of the stream.

## 2026-10-27

### Added
- Halloween night, Oct 27 – Nov 1: houses go dark at 9 PM ICT and a friendly ghost walks the roads.
  Tagged `halloween-festival`.

## 2026-10-26

### Changed
- The action column shows only in the 1F hall.
- The 1F censer is open, with a bed of ash.
- Repeated static parts of the grounds are drawn as instanced meshes, for speed.

### Fixed
- The field hut moved out of the fuel station forecourt.
- The main stupa's door gables sit flat to the wall.
